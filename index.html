Chi-Square — Quick Reference

For our purposes df = 3 almost always (df = # of genotypes - 1) in problems

RECALL: x^2 = SUM [(actual-expected)^2/(expected)]

ON CHI-SQUARE CHART (p value)

p > 0.05  →  fail to reject null hypothesis, differences is due to chance, genes are unlinked  →  independent assortment
p = 0.05  →  reject null hypothesis, differences is NOT due to chance, genes are linked
p < 0.05  →  reject null hypothesis, differences is NOT due to chance, genes are linked

TYPE 1 error definition:
A type 1 error is when you reject a null hypothesis, conclude the differences are NOT due to chance and that the genes are LINKED when in fact, the differences WERE due to chance and the genes are actually UNLINKED.

Recombination Frequency
RECALL: r = (# of recombinants)/(# total population)
r > 0.5  →  not possible but if somehow you get that answer anywhere  →  unlinked, independent assortment
r = 0.5  →  genes are unlinked, independent assortment
r < 0.5  →  genes are linked
Genetic Distance

FOR LINKED GENES, distance [cM], centimorgan,

distance = (r * 100) cM
